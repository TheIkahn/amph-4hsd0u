> [!note] 📜 History
> Originally a minor shrine to [[Mnestia]], built during the Second Cycle of Grace. It was decommissioned two centuries ago, when the goddess's rites were consolidated into the Marmoreal Palace. Mnestia's faithful don't tear down their own places of worship, so the building was kept. It has stood mostly empty for decades: used now and then for overflow storage, and once, briefly, as a quarantine annex during a plague year. Aglaea held it in reserve, maintained but unassigned, waiting for the right occupants.

> [!abstract] 🏠 Rooms & features
> - **12 rooms.** Nine can be private quarters; three are unassigned.
> - **Central atrium.**
> - **Training hall** in the basement, built for full-power fights.
> - **Forge annex** for crafting.
> - **Glass dome** connected directly to the light device of the statue.
> - **Teleslate booster** (5g).
> - **Private garden** with fruit trees and three open plots for planting. Zuzros has been tending it; Kenkai and Franzen replanted it in Session 38.
> - **Sealed subcellar**, contents unknown, "for later discussion".
