*Location · Fallen city of Janus*

> [!quote] At a glance
> The fallen holy city of Janus, corrupted by the Abyss and swallowed by the Evernight. Much of the old city is now cleansed. Below it lie the Temple of the Three Fates and Tartarus.

> [!info] ◉ At a glance
> | | |
> | :-- | :-- |
> | **Tied to** | [[Tribios]] (its former Holy Maiden) and [[Castorice]] (found by its River of Souls) |
> | **Titan** | [[Janus]], dead since Session 27 |
> | **State** | Upper sectors largely cleansed. The lower strata are still deep in the Evernight |
> | **Notable places** | The River of Souls and its soul trees, the Passage Gates, the Temple of the Three Fates, the Horologion, Tartarus |

> [!note] 📜 What we know
> - The Abyss corrupted the city, and the Evernight covers much of it.
> - Much of Old Janusopolis was cleansed when the party and the Chrysos Heirs fought the anchors plaguing the land. The petrified Furiae warriors were freed and regained their free will.
> - The freed Furiae are scattered through the cleared sectors, mostly traumatized and missing memories. Some made it back to [[Castrum Kremnos]].
> - Janus's vault lies in the Temple of the Three Fates. Tribios took his coreflame there, and Janus died.
> - Tartarus lies beneath the temple: a labyrinth of doors, vaults of Zagreus, and stranger things.

> [!tip] ◎ People
> - **[[Jossum]]** (Allied): his Skyfire troop is posted on the Okhema–Janusopolis border.
> - **[[Tribios]]** (Allied): leads expeditions into the ruins.
> - **[[Tobari]]** (Neutral): met in the lower city fighting the Abyssal Seam-Mother.
> - **Metanoros:** tried to stop us guiding Tribios to her coreflame. Kenkai talked him into walking to Okhema.

> [!example]- 🎲 History with the party
> - **Sessions 1–3.** We arrived in Amphoreus from Janusopolis.
> - **Sessions 5–7.** Expedition to find Castorice by the River of Souls.
> - **Sessions 14–17.** The Abyssal Nexus: the party broke tethers while the Heirs struck the main anchor.
> - **Alpha Behir hunt.** Cleared a Behir den hindering relief efforts in the newly freed section. The Skyfire warriors later found it empty, possibly without knowing it was us.
> - **Sessions 25–27.** Escorted Tribios to her coreflame in the Temple of the Three Fates.
> - **Sessions 28–32.** Descended into Tartarus: the vaults of Zagreus, the Mystic Forge, the corrupted Furiae Praetors, then fled when Death came knocking.
> - **Sessions 34–37.** Fought the Abyssal Seam-Mother with Tobari, then followed the ring of sigils to the Horologion and found the black glass disc.
> - **Session 38.** Returned to Tartarus.
