> [!note] 📜 What we know
> - A **Black Tide** washes over parts of the land and corrupts it, inviting evil in its wake. All we know of its origin is that it came from the sky (Session 24).
> - The **Evernight** is anything taken by the Abyss or the Black Tide. Some places have been swallowed by it.
> - All the Titans are corrupted to some degree (Session 24).
> - The **Chrysos Heirs** act as protectors and celebrities, helping civilians where they can. Legend says the twelve Heirs will one day unite and vanquish the Black Tide forever.
> - The **Flame-Chase**, the Heirs' quest to claim the coreflames, has begun again (Session 24). Phainon is the Deliverer and will claim his last.
> - Something other than the Black Tide keeps anyone from leaving Amphoreus (Tribios, Session 24).

> [!quote] 📜 Lore & sayings
> Amphoreus turns on twelve Titans, each bound to a great Idea. The month you were born under tells people more about you than you might like.

> All twelve Titans of Amphoreus have their own Titankin that are built for their beliefs.

> The Flame-Chase of Amphoreus states that 12 legendary heroes will slay the Titans of the land and take up their mantle, ushering in a new Era Nova and cleansing their world of all that is evil.

> The [[A_Travelers_Guide_to_the_Light_Calendar|Light Calendar]] belongs to the Titans. Gate opens the year for [[Janus]], Strife sits near its end for [[Nikador]], and Fortune closes it for [[Zagreus]]. Plan your feasts accordingly.
>
> Leave one serving of [[Thanatos]]' Requiem Pudding untouched at the table. The dead are guests too, and they notice the slight.
>
> Pour the first spoonful of [[Phagousa]]'s Laughter onto the ground before you eat. The Tide takes her share first, always.
>
> When a Chrysos Heir hands you a home and a purse of coins, it is rarely only generosity. Take it anyway. Just keep your eyes open.
>
> The true nature and origin of Amphoreus is unknown.
