*Location · City of scholars*

> [!quote] At a glance
> The scholars' city built on and around Cerces' Sacred Tree, home to seven schools of thought and to Anaxagoras.

> [!quote] 📜 Lore
> The Grove of Epiphany grows on and around Cerces' Sacred Tree. Seven schools argue beneath its branches, and the Head Sage's chair is left empty on purpose.
>
> The Grove keeps seven schools of thought, from the rigorous Nousporists to the Erythrokeramists, who swear you can taste a sound if you only stop thinking so hard.
