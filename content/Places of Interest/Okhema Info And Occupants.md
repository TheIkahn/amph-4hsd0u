*Location · City · Seat of the Chrysos Heirs*

> [!quote] At a glance
> A crowded, growing city and the heart of the Chrysos Heirs' power, watched over by the colossal statue of Kephale. It's been the party's home base since we arrived in Amphoreus.

> [!info] ◉ At a glance
> | | |

> [!abstract] 🏛️ Places in Okhema
> - **The Metaxy:** the large place we arrived in, reached by travelling through the drift. Crafted by the Titans. Its gates now let us travel quickly across Amphoreus.
> - **The Tri-Lamp house:** our first lodging. Aglaea's maids kept house; we never learned who ran it.
> - **The Marmoreal Palace:** a vast white-marble hall the Heirs use for big events, its walls carved with murals of the Titans. Holds the war room.
> - **The Court of Seasons:** near the inner city and the World Titan's frozen frame.
> - **The Dressmaster's Quarters:** Aglaea's weaving chambers, where she makes boss weapons.
> - **The forge:** run by [[Kiron]].
> - **Skyfire HQ:** deep in the city near the palace.
> - **The statue of Kephale:** a giant figure holding up the planet with his hands.

> [!tip] ◎ People

> - **[[Ione Kalyx]]** (Allied): Skyfire lieutenant who brought us here.
> - **[[Jossum]]** (Allied): Skyfire officer and friend.
> - **[[Commander Deva]]** (Allied): leader of the Skyfire Dragons.
> - **[[Kiron]]** (Allied): forgemaster. His workers Damar (elf), Liisa (human) and Pell (dragonborn, his apprentice) were our first job.
> - **[[Thessil Vane]]** (Neutral): head chairman of the civic engineering committee.
> - **[[Caenis]]** (Opposed): speaker against Aglaea.
> - **[[Linnilae]]** (Allied): our staff at the Philoxenion.

> [!example]- 🎲 History with the party
> - **Arrival.** Ione Kalyx led us out of the Metaxy and through a cheering crowd to registration and the Tri-Lamp house.
> - **The Heirs' tower.** Aglaea gave us a passport for the city's sectioned areas and sent us to Janusopolis to find Castorice. Mydeimos left to look into items stolen from Castrum Kremnos.
> - **Kiron's crane job.** We protected Damar, Liisa and Pell during deconstruction work.
> - **The Marmoreal Palace.** Formal dinner with the Heirs, where we met Phainon, Commander Deva and Thessil Vane.
> - **Tribios's briefing.** She showed us a giant mass of corruption on a map. We took the smaller tethers while the Heirs handled the main mass.
> - **Session 18.** Aglaea gave us the Philoxenion.
> - **Session 24.** Aglaea's public address announced the new Flame-Chase.
