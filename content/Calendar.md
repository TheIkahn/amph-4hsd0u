The year in Amphoreus runs on the Light Calendar, Kephale's gift to humanity: twelve months of 31 days, each kept by a Titan, in four divine seasons. The week starts on **Eosdas** and rests on **Selenodas**, running straight on from one month into the next. Gold days are the ones the party has lived through; click one to see which sessions happened then.

For the hours of the day and the Astrean names, see [[A_Travelers_Guide_to_the_Light_Calendar|A Traveler's Guide to the Light Calendar]].
