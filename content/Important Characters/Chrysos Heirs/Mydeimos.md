> [!abstract] 🎭 Appearance & Personality
> **Appearance:** Blonde-red hair, orange eyes, red flame-like markings imbued into his skin. Gold armour on his lower half with blue accents, and matching jewellery. Carries a bident. Three to four inches taller than Yuichi.
>
> **Personality:** Rowdy but tempered. Proud of his strength. Blunt, brutal, and loyal to his own code.

> [!note] 📜 Background & History
> **Known history:** Born in Castrum Kremnos, a nation that worships Nikador and believes conflict is the highest form of truth. It is called the "Capital of Might" and has earned the name "Bloodbathed Battlefront" for its constant warfare. Mydeimos was recognized as an Heir after single-handedly defending a border fort against an Abyss incursion for three days.
>
> **Prophecy:**
> > "The undying Mydeimos, the lion apart from the rest. O Chrysos Heir that seeks the Coreflame of Strife, you must suffer a thousand deaths, be bathed in blood on the path home, and bear the madness of fate alone, for one must slay a god to become one. Iron-hooves pound across the wilderness for the campaign, and must eventually soak in the blood of their homeland."

> [!tip] 🤝 Relationships
> **Allies / Friends:** [[Aglaea]], [[Phainon]], [[Castorice]], [[Tribios]].
>
> **Enemies / Rivals:** The Black Tide, the Evernight.

> [!example]- 🎲 Session Log
> - **Session 4.** First meeting, beside Aglaea. Leaves to deal with weapons stolen from Castrum Kremnos.
> - **Session 12 (A Dinner with the Heirs).** Loses an arm-wrestle to Yuichi 2-0 without trying hard, then challenges him to a spar. Enters his Vendetta stance (a bone throne rises behind him) and launches Yuichi 350 ft; Caledor catches him. He and Phainon then challenge the whole party.
> - **Session 13.** Fights the party alongside Phainon and throws Kenkai into Caledor. Suggests Hyacinthia as a good fit for Franzen and Velkhi. Agrees to train anyone who needs it, starting at 5 a.m.
> - **Session 17.** Arrives with Phainon after the Nexus fight.
> - **Session 33.** Relaxing in the indoor springs with Phainon. Will travel with the party on the next Flame-Chase, and agrees to train Yuichi further.
