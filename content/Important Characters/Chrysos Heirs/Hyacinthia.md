> [!abstract] 🎭 Appearance & Personality
> **Appearance:** Pink, cutesy hair. Red frilly attire lined with gold emblems. Travels with a small horse in the same style that the party has nicknamed "Fat Fuck".

> **Personality:** Cutesy and outgoing, a real ray of sunshine. Quick to reach out and help whenever possible.

> [!note] 📜 Background & History
> **Known history:** Born in the Grove of Epiphany, sky-bound settlements that drift above Amphoreus. A Nousporist (dream-walker, streamer of visions), beloved by the common people for her public broadcasts. Playful and optimistic, she believes the Heirs' role is to inspire hope, not just fight.
>
> **Prophecy:**
> > "As the city-state in the clouds crumbles through time, the Twilight Courtyard opens its gates once more, bringing a glimmer of light to Evernight. You, Physician Hyacinthia, Chrysos Heir who guards the Sky Coreflame, must inherit your ancestors' will and mend the torn fabric of dusk and dawn. May the rainbow light pour down, dissolve all grudges, and bring the dawn back to this land."
