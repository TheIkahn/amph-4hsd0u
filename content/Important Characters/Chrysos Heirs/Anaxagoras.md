*Character · NPC · Chrysos Heir*


> [!quote] At a glance
> The "blasphemer" Heir of Cerces. A prodigy scholar who runs Okhema's research from the Grove, argues that the Titans aren't gods, and refuses to claim his coreflame. Do not call him "Anaxa".

> [!abstract] 🎭 Appearance & Personality
> **Appearance:** Blue-green hair, blue and black layered coat, red jewellery. A blindfold over his left eye and a red sigil on his right hand. Carries a gun and flaunts it without caution.
>
> **Personality:** Knowledgeable, pragmatic, short fuse. Cold and precise. Claims not to care about loving himself, and holds love in disdain.

> [!note] 📜 Background & History
> **Known history:** A prodigy scholar recognized as an Heir at age 12. He oversees Okhema's research institutions as a sort of absentee father, and is rumoured to be working on a "grand theory" that will explain the Abyss and how to stop it for good. Rarely leaves the Grove. Taught Phainon and Ulixes.
>
> **Prophecy:**
> > "The Grove of Epiphany, where knowledge flourishes and philosophers are born. Yet here stands Anaxagoras the blasphemer, the Chrysos Heir who challenges the Coreflame of Reason: would you truly embrace infamy and defy prophecy, driving the thorns of doubt into the Sacred Tree of wisdom? 'Ridiculous. In a world full of lies, I am the only truth.'"

> [!tip] 🤝 Relationships
> **Students:** [[Phainon]], Ulixes.
>
> **Enemies / Rivals:** Doesn't like [[Aglaea]], and the feeling is mutual.

> [!example]- 🎲 Session Log
> - **Session 19 (An Evening at the Grove).** Lectures the party on Nousporism, opening with "what is the difference between a belief and a conclusion you have made?" Franzen and Caerwyn challenge his disdain for love. His thesis: the Titans are not gods, just powerful beings, though not without divinity. Yuichi tells him he's killing more people by not claiming his coreflame. He reports that Cipher is nowhere to be found and Hysilens is in Styxia drinking herself away. Afterwards he tells Mayuri he hasn't found anything about her ailment yet and suggests she ask Castorice.
> - **Session 24.** Yuichi brings him up with Aglaea, which sets her off.

> [!info]- 📌 Trivia / Facts
> - Hates being called "Anaxa". Ulixes warned Yuichi about it.
> - Ulixes says he'll make rash decisions in pursuit of the truth.
