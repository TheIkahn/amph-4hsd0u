*Character · NPC · Chrysos Heir · Coreflame retrieved*


> [!quote] At a glance
> The Holy Maiden of Janusopolis and Heir of Janus. The first to take a coreflame in the new Flame-Chase, with the party's help. Mayuri calls her "Tribbie".

> [!abstract] 🎭 Appearance & Personality
> **Appearance:** Red hair, blue eyes, white dress trimmed with gold and decorated with flowers, and a matching flower headband.
>
> **Personality:** Pragmatic and tireless, haunted by the loss of her city. Openly nervous before claiming her coreflame.

> [!note] 📜 Background & History
> **Known history:** Former Holy Maiden and high priestess of the Passage Gates in Janusopolis before the city fell. She now leads expeditions into the ruins, searching for survivors, relics, and the truth of what caused the Black Tide surge.
>
> **Prophecy:**
> > "The Messenger is destined to depart from that holy land beloved by the tripartite prophecy, splitting herself into a thousand pieces and setting off on her journey. O Tribios, Holy Maiden of Janusopolis, the Chrysos Heir guarding the Coreflame of Passage. You shall toil for the masses, bringing the news of deliverance to all domains. Go, and find the children of humanity with golden blood in their veins, shatter the dimmest dark in this world, and walk toward the tomorrow where the stars gleam."

> [!tip] 🤝 Relationships
> **Allies / Friends:** [[Aglaea]], [[Mydeimos]], [[Castorice]], [[Phainon]].
>
> **Party:** Mayuri called her "Tribbie" a year ago and still slips into it (Session 14).
>
> **Enemies / Rivals:** None listed.

> [!example]- 🎲 Session Log
> - **Session 13.** Her scouting finds a structural source of the void that the Heirs believe can be destroyed.
> - **Session 14.** Sends the briefing for the Nexus tethers (Stratum IV) and leads it at the Marmoreal Palace war room.
> - **Session 17.** Times the Heirs' strike on the main tether to land within seconds of the party's.
> - **Session 20.** Invites everyone to Aglaea's address.
> - **Session 24.** Announced as the first to take a coreflame, with Janusopolis as the first door. Tells the party that something other than the Black Tide stops anyone from leaving Amphoreus.
> - **Session 25.** The party escorts her into Janusopolis. She uses her aura against the Furiae.
> - **Session 26.** Reaches the Temple of the Three Fates and confirms the vault is Janus's. Calls the star in the centre the guiding star, since Janus was the first Titan.
> - **Session 27.** Recovers to finish the Evernight beast with her ultimate. Claims her coreflame; the party hears cryptic messages telling them to kill the gods and nourish the land with their blood. Janus dies. She cries out for her mother and falls unconscious, and Phainon carries her out.

> [!info]- 📌 Trivia / Facts
> - Her prophecy calls her the Messenger.
> - Janus died when she took his coreflame (Pyrodas, 25 Month of Joy, 4931).
