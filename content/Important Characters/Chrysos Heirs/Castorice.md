*Character · NPC · Chrysos Heir*


> [!quote] At a glance
> Daughter of the River of Souls and Heir of Thanatos. Quiet, sleepy and prophetic, more at ease among the dead than the living. Anyone who touches her normally dies, but Mayuri didn't.

> [!abstract] 🎭 Appearance & Personality
> **Appearance:** White-purple hair, frilly flower-and-butterfly dress with a matching headpiece, a bandage-like top, bows everywhere. Wields a scythe. Turns into butterflies to travel.
>
> **Personality:** Very mild. Speaks calmly, often without much tone. Often sleepy, and a heavy sleeper. Dislikes crowds.

> [!note] 📜 Background & History
> **Known history:** Little is known. Records say she was "found" near the River of Souls in Janusopolis as a child, already bearing the mark of an Heir. She doesn't speak of her past, though she told Mayuri she is from [[Aidonia]], a land far to the north that worships Death (Session 12).
>
> **Prophecy:**
> > "The land that reveres death, Aidonia, where snow falls endlessly, has today drifted into a sweet slumber. Castorice, daughter of the River of Souls, Chrysos Heir in search of the 'Death' Coreflame, sets forth. You must guard the lament of souls and embrace the solitude of destiny. Life and death are but a journey. When the butterfly alights on the branch, what withers will bloom anew."

> [!tip] 🤝 Relationships
> **Allies / Friends:** [[Aglaea]], [[Mydeimos]], [[Phainon]], [[Tribios]].
>
> **Party:** Bonded with Mayuri over views and avoiding crowds (Session 12). Told Sir Caerwyn to come to her when he reaches the end of his road (Session 24).
>
> **Enemies / Rivals:** The Black Tide, the Evernight.

> [!example]- 🎲 Session Log
> - **Session 4.** Aglaea sends the party to find her after she stops reporting back.
> - **Session 5.** Jossum says she was last seen days ago and likes to rest by the soul trees along the River of Souls.
> - **Session 7.** Found asleep by a tree after killing the local Behirs. Sora wakes her with water. She says her dreams are why she sleeps so heavily, then turns into butterflies and heads home.
> - **Session 12 (A Dinner with the Heirs).** Sits at the window and talks with Mayuri. Both felt calm, almost familiar, near the pollenpump.
> - **Session 13.** Leaves the spar early for the library with Mayuri and Sora.
> - **Session 19.** Anaxagoras suggests Mayuri ask Castorice about her ailment.
> - **Session 24.** Sees the same visions of the psychopomp that Mayuri does, and says she can see the end of Okhema. Offers Mayuri her hand, and Mayuri survives the touch. Can't sense Mayuri's death (Mayuri is "fractured") or Caerwyn's (he already died in a past life). Mayuri's codex shows a private conversation Castorice had about Aglaea, which leaves her dumbfounded.

> [!info]- 📌 Trivia / Facts
> - Feels most comfortable in Janusopolis, beside the River of Souls.
> - Her aura only activates in moments of distress.
> - The party's ring of sigils was found on the trip to rescue her.
