*Character · NPC · Chrysos Heir · Coreflame retrieved*


> [!quote] At a glance
> Aglaea the Goldweaver, Heir of Mnestia and Dressmaster of Okhema. A thousand-year-old demigod who runs Okhema, summoned the party, and gave us the Philoxenion as our home.

> [!abstract] 🎭 Appearance & Personality
> **Appearance:** Blonde hair, blue-green eyes, white dress with golden accents, frilled with bows and ribbons. Decorative olive branches in her hair. Usually attended by headless mannequin maids that carry out tasks for her.
>
> **Personality:** Soft-spoken, outreaching, helpful by nature. Firm about her priorities: she told Mayuri her mission is the safety of Okhema and that we had best not end up on the other side of it.

> [!note] 📜 Background & History
> **Known history:** Born in Okhema to a family of weavers. Recognized as an Heir at what was believed to be age 16, when she wove the first "golden thread", a material that can mend Titan-era relics and influence fate itself. She has served as Okhema's spiritual guide and the Council's mentor for over 70 years. She doesn't know where her powers come from, but believes they stem from her bloodline's blessing from Mnestia.
>
> **Prophecy:**
> > "Aglaea the Goldweaver, the Chrysos Heir bearing the Coreflame of 'Romance'... You shall summon the heroes of this world, and lead them to once again embark on an endless journey. Where they will smite down the gods, return the divine fire, and grant rebirth to the dying land of Amphoreus."

> [!tip] 🤝 Relationships
> **Allies / Friends:** [[Mydeimos]], [[Phainon]], [[Castorice]], [[Tribios]], [[Jossum]]. In contact with [[Cipher]].
>
> **Enemies / Rivals:** The Black Tide, the Evernight, [[Anaxagoras]] (mentioning him "starts a fire" in her). [[Caenis]] opposes her at every turn.

> [!example]- 🎲 Session Log
> - **Session 4.** Meets the party with Mydeimos and sends us to Janusopolis to find Castorice.
> - **Session 7.** Waiting with Castorice when we return. Promises the party private quarters.
> - **Session 12 (A Dinner with the Heirs).** Shares a love of pickled fish with Franzen. In her private quarters she shows us spirit essence and offers to reshape the souls we bring her, unlocking Transposition (soul forging).
> - **Session 13.** Judges the sparring match against Mydeimos and Phainon and names Yuichi and Caledor winners. Says we've done more in one week than most do in a month.
> - **Session 18.** Takes us to her weaving chambers (the Dressmaster's Quarters, where she makes boss weapons). After dinner at the palace she gives us the [[Philoxenion]], a former shrine to Mnestia, as our home.
> - **Session 24 (An Address to Okhema).** Gives the public address announcing that the Flame-Chase has begun again and that Phainon is the Deliverer. Afterwards she tells the party there are enemies in the city who want the Flame-Chase too, and confirms she is a demigod.
> - **Session 25.** Mayuri shows her the codex. Aglaea identifies the private conversation in it as one from about 100 years ago, finds Mayuri's power strange, and asks to see any new conversations that appear.
> - **Session 33.** Has no word yet on the next Flame-Chase or on the person tied to Yuichi.

> [!info]- 📌 Trivia / Facts
> - Runs Okhema politically.
> - One of her favourite foods is pickled fish.
> - Her family is blessed by Mnestia.
> - Her physical sight is dim, but she sees and hears through threads woven throughout Okhema. Phainon warned that she can hear what we say.
> - Can generate items from creatures' essences after they die, and offers this as a service to the party.
