> [!note] 📜 Background & History
> **Known history:** She received her coreflame on an expedition and then went missing. Some believe Cipher is several people; others think she is one person who moves through identities like clothing. Historical texts say she did something significant involving the Dawn Device, but the details are unclear or deliberately obscured.
