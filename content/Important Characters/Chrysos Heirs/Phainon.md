*Character · NPC · Chrysos Heir · The Deliverer*


> [!quote] At a glance
> Phainon of Aedes Elysiae, Heir of Kephale and the Deliverer of legend. He will be the last to claim his coreflame and is meant to lead Amphoreus into a new dawn.

> [!abstract] 🎭 Appearance & Personality
> **Appearance:** White hair, blue-green eyes, draped in layers of cloth that flow in the wind. Wields a 7 ft sword called Dawnmaker that he summons at will.
>
> **Personality:** Calm and tempered, friendly and outreaching. Curious about the party. Nervous in front of crowds, and says he doesn't care about legends of his name, only that everyone is okay in the end.

> [!note] 📜 Background & History
> **Known history:** Grew up in Aedes Elysiae, a remote, isolated settlement high in the mountains. Came down to Okhema after being recognized as an Heir and now serves as the "bridge" between Kephale's legacy and the mortal world. Studied under Anaxagoras alongside Ulixes.
>
> **Prophecy:**
> > "Nameless hero, the Chrysos Heir carrying the Coreflame of 'Worldbearing': you must memorize the ideals of all worlds, carry the fate of multitudes, and bring the first light of dawn to the new world. But should dawn have never existed, let the fires of rage burn this body to ashes and transform into the blazing sun of tomorrow!"

>
> **Teacher:** [[Anaxagoras]].
>
> **Enemies / Rivals:** The Black Tide, the Evernight.

> [!example]- 🎲 Session Log
> - **Session 12 (A Dinner with the Heirs).** Introduced at the Marmoreal Palace. Beats Caledor at arm-wrestling 2-0, then knocks him out in one blow in the ring. Hands out Teleslates.
> - **Session 13.** Spars the party with Mydeimos in Dawnbreaker Stance, and convinces Kenkai to betray Caledor mid-fight.
> - **Session 14.** Picks up on Mayuri calling Tribios "Tribbie".
> - **Session 18.** Gives the newer party members their Teleslates.
> - **Session 24 (An Address to Okhema).** Nervous before his introduction as the Deliverer; the party talks him through it.
> - **Session 25.** Leads the party into Janusopolis by Metaxy. Recognizes a symbol in Mayuri's codex as the one in his eyes. Cuts a portal through the Metaxy straight to Tribios, and holds Dawnmaker aloft for an hour so its light keeps the Evernight off us.
> - **Session 26.** Mutters "the Black Tide" at a carving, and lights the braziers of Janus's vault. His blade reacts to a Dawnspeaker of Kephale.
> - **Session 27.** Carries the unconscious Tribios out after Janus dies. Warns that the third Fate Titan may be inside the temple.
> - **Session 33.** Pleased to hear Terravox is heading home and suggests a welcoming party. Says Mydeimos will join our next Flame-Chase.

> [!info]- 📌 Trivia / Facts
> - Has more willpower than any other current Chrysos Heir.
> - Dislikes being watched by crowds.
> - Dawnmaker can make a 90 ft light in which the Evernight can't harm anyone.
> - Will be the last Heir to claim his coreflame.
> - The Dawnspeaker in Session 26 said Kephale willingly gave up his coreflame.
