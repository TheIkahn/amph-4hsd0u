> [!note] 📜 Background & History
> **Known history:** Rose from the farming communities in Amphoreus' eastern territories. Recognized as an Heir after quelling a catastrophic earthquake that threatened several villages. A champion of the common people, he believes the Heirs' duty is to protect the land itself, not just its cities.
