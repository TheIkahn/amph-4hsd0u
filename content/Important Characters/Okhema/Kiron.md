*Character · NPC · Forgemaster of Okhema*


> [!quote] At a glance
> Okhema's 12 ft forgemaster, who forged the Chrysos Heirs' weapons. Quiet, monotone, and fond of a drink. Mayuri can lift his giant hammer.

> [!abstract] 🎭 Appearance & Personality
> **Appearance:** Dark skin, huge and extremely tall (12 ft). Greek-style pauldron and armband armour. Wields a giant hammer.
>
> **Personality:** Low, monotone voice; not very expressive. Likes to drink.

> [!note] 📜 Background & History
> **Motivations:** Running what may be Okhema's only forge.

> [!tip] 🤝 Relationships
> **Allies / Friends:** His workers Damar (elf), Liisa (human) and Pell (dragonborn apprentice). Father of [[Linnilae]].

> [!example]- 🎲 Session Log
> - **Session 4.** Meets the party and sends us to guard Damar, Liisa and Pell while they use a large crane for deconstruction work.
> - **Session 11.** Congratulates us on how hard we've fought and shows us our rewards. Mayuri picks up his giant hammer and wields it with ease, to everyone's shock.

> [!info]- 📌 Trivia / Facts
> - Forged the weapons wielded by the Chrysos Heirs.
> - 12 ft tall.
