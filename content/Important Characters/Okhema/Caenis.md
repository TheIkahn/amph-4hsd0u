> [!abstract] 🎭 Appearance & Personality
> **Appearance:** Dark skin, yellow eyes, tall, with scarred arms. White dress; carries a staff.
>
> **Personality:** Rough, stern, tempered.

> [!note] 📜 Background & History
> **Motivations:** Dislikes how Okhema defers to Aglaea, and despises her for a fate that she believes will make Aglaea abandon Okhema to chase her coreflame. Public addresses in Okhema are given either by her or by Aglaea.

> [!tip] 🤝 Relationships
> **Rivals:** [[Aglaea]]. Sees the party as Aglaea's people, and so as rivals.
>
> **Contacts:** Swapped Teleslate details with Caledor.

> [!example]- 🎲 Session Log
> - **Session 24 (An Address to Okhema).** Caledor finds her stewing in a corner. She complains that Aglaea only cares about the Heirs, then gets annoyed to realize she's talking to Aglaea's helpers. Predicts the Black Tide will consume Okhema while Aglaea flees on the Flame-Chase; when Yuichi asks if that's the world she wants, she says no one will live in it. Says stopping Aglaea will take bureaucracy, and trades Teleslates with Caledor. When she rises during the address, she meets Caledor's eye and thinks better of it.

> [!question]- ❓ Open Questions
> - What is she planning to do about Aglaea?
