*Character · NPC*


> [!quote] At a glance
> An ancient nine-tailed kitsune with a vine-wrapped greatsword, exiled from Kahnmae Forest like Yuichi. He fought beside the party against the Abyssal Seam-Mother, then turned down our offer to travel together and set off for Okhema.

> [!abstract] 🎭 Appearance & Personality
> **Appearance:** Long grey hair, shirtless, velvet pants and wraps. Yellow vines and flowers run down his arm and onto his greatsword. Two pins in his hair. His shadow shows nine fox tails and ears.
>
> **Personality:** Determined and straightforward. Polite but firm: he turned down the party's offer to travel together without any back-and-forth.
>
> **Fighting style:** Keeps his distance from the party and fights on his own terms. Uses his greatsword in flurries of blows. Heals constantly: wounds from the Evernight close almost as soon as they open.

> [!note] 📜 Background & History
> **Known history**
> - Long ago he was banished from Kahnmae Forest, Yuichi's homeland. Like Yuichi, he is an exile from it.
> - Has wandered since in search of strength.
> - Came to Amphoreus through some kind of gateway, a situation not unlike the party's own.
>
> **Motivations**
> - Searching for the Flame-Chase, according to the Session 36 conversation.
> - Heading for Okhema. The party gave him directions and told him he could use the Metaxy to shorten the trip.
> - Earlier notes say he has disdain for the Flame-Chase. Why he seeks it anyway is unknown.

> [!tip] 🤝 Relationships
> **Allies / Friends**
> - **The party.** Fought alongside us against the skitterlings and the Abyssal Seam-Mother. Friendly, but declined to join.
> - **Sir Caerwyn.** Held the battle line beside him against the Seam-Mother.
>
> **Ties**
> - **Yuichi.** Fellow exile of Kahnmae Forest. Mayuri sensed a presence tied to Yuichi arrive in Amphoreus (Session 25), and Yuichi had been looking for that person since. Tobari turned out to be them.
>
> **Enemies / Rivals:** None known.

> [!example]- 🎲 Session Log
> - **Session 25 (The Dark Descent), flashback.** Mayuri tells Yuichi she felt a presence connected to him enter Amphoreus. Yuichi gets nervous and says he can't focus on it right now.
> - **Session 33 (A Quiet Day in Okhema).** Aglaea has no information yet that could help locate the person tied to Yuichi.
> - **Session 34 (Swallowed Whole).** On the way down from the Metaxy beside the Janusopolis temple, the party is attacked by skitterlings and an abyss beast. Mid-fight, a figure closes in from the distance: the presence Mayuri felt, the person Yuichi was looking for.
> - **Session 35 (The Seam-Mother).** Tobari joins the fight against the skitterlings. An Abyssal Seam-Mother bursts out of the ground. The party notices that Evernight damage on Tobari heals over as fast as it tears him apart.
> - **Session 36 (The Seam-Mother Pt. 2).** Tobari keeps his distance and makes repeated flurries with his sword. He stops the Seam-Mother's moltings from ripening into her brood, holds the line with Caerwyn, and lands two devastating hits on the abyss beast she "births". After the fight, Yuichi and Kenkai (with Franzen chiming in) talk with him and learn about his exile and the gateway. He declines to join the party, takes directions to Okhema and the Metaxy, and leaves.

> [!info]- 📌 Trivia / Facts
> - His nine tails and ears show only in his shadow.
> - In common kitsune lore, nine tails mark great age and power. That fits what he said, but it isn't confirmed for Amphoreus.
> - He is the second Kahnmae Forest exile the party has met.
