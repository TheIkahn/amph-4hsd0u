## 🔥 Heroic Saga of the Flame-Chase

> [!danger] The Flame-Chase
> **Giver:** [[Aglaea]] · **Location:** [[Amphoreus Info|Amphoreus]]
>
> At Dawncloud, before about seventy thousand people, Aglaea announced that the Flame-Chase has begun again: the Black Tide isn't slowing, [[Phainon]] is the Deliverer, and [[Tribios]] would take the first step through the first door, Janusopolis. People often die on the Chase, and she asked the city to stand with her. Afterwards she warned that enemies in the city want the Flame-Chase for themselves, and Tribios said no one may leave Amphoreus, for a reason other than the Black Tide: walk to the very end and there's nothing at the edge. Aglaea has no word yet on where the Chase goes next, but [[Mydeimos]] is coming with us when it moves.
>
> - [x] Sit on the council at Dawncloud for Aglaea's address.
> - [x] Walk with Tribios to the Temple of the Three Fates and see Passage claimed.
> - [ ] Learn what Janus's last words demand.
> - [ ] Find the enemies in Okhema who mean to take the Flame-Chase for themselves.
> - [ ] Learn why no one can leave Amphoreus.
> - [ ] Learn whose red cloth lay before the Temple of the Three Fates.
> - [ ] Answer the call when the Chase moves again.
> - [ ] Stand with the Deliverer to the end of the Chase.
>
> **Janus's last words**
> > *Overthrow the gods. Return the coreflames.*
> > *Inherit divine authority. Forge godly miracles.*
> > *Go kill. Go mourn.*
> > *Kill my kin. Mourn our fate.*
> > *Ferry the spirits of the gods. Nourish the land with divine essence.*
> > *Yet, thou shalt remember. Thou must remember.*
> > *All shall bid farewell to one, and that person alone will witness the miracle.*
> > *Such... is destiny.*

> [!danger] The Descent Beneath the Temple
> **Location:** Beneath the Temple of the Three Fates
>
> When Tribios took Passage, [[Janus]] died and a doorway opened beneath his golden blood. Phainon carried Tribios home, marked the way back to the Metaxy, and warned that the third Fate Titan may be somewhere in the temple. Below is a colosseum ringed with doors: one chained shut that opens only for bones, another to a labyrinth that hasn't been empty in a very long time. When the last Furiae Praetor fell, Death came knocking and we got out to Okhema.
>
> - [x] Follow the doorway beneath Janus's golden blood.
> - [x] Speak with the [[Weightmaster]].
> - [x] Open the vault of Zagreus with the Hand of Zagreus.
> - [x] Destroy the Magical Forge.
> - [x] Defeat the Furiae Praetors at the pylon and claim the Zagreus Chest.
> - [x] Make it back to Okhema.
> - [x] Bring bones to the chained door.
> - [ ] Learn whether the third Fate Titan waits within the temple.
> - [ ] **Recurring:** delve into the Temple of the Three Fates. *Journal: back in Tartarus in Session 38.*

Everyone has a story about it, and Delmore's notebook is filling fast.
>
