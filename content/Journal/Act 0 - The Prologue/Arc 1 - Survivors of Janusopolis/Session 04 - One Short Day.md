*Arc 1: Survivors of Janusopolis (Session 1-7)*

Astardas, 6 Phareon, 1E 808 - Wevdas, 7 Phareon, 1E 808
Kenkai got the MVP award.

we hop off the train to Okhema and immediately notice the warm hues and colors. A huge crowd is waiting for us as they are amazed to how we were able to survive. Kalyx is guiding us through the crowd when we get to registration. Sora was already written down. we are being escorted to the tri-lamp house, a guest house with 12 rooms. A man with a large purple hat then greets us. a messenger will come by in a couple of hours to grab us. someone special wants to have a word with us. after a while a cleric comes by to heal Franzen of his deafness. Yuichi who was asleep awaken by an inorganic autonomous maid and everyone is freaked out by it. 

Mayuri talks to Caledor about what he wanted to discuss earlier. Caledor pipes up and grills into Mayuri about how she knew all of them before hand. Mayuri confused about what he means by this as she remembers things a bit different although hazy that she knew all of them about a year ago. this perplexes everyone as they do not believe Mayuri as they did not know themselves even a year ago. Mayuri talks about keeping a journal around as her memory has gone bad. However, it is indecipherable even to mayuri as she tries to read it. she talks about how Franzen and Kenkai are on “The Hunt” and how kenkai was scared by a Kirin. Yuichi walks in and is even more frustrated at these “convenient” excuses and thinks that Mayuri knows more than she’s letting on. He is frustrated that Mayuri went off on his own and broke rank. Mayuri takes this as an insult thinking that Yuichi does not believe that Mayuri cannot handle her own putting it on her age and frame.

in the meanwhile Kenkai is approached by two kids selling their pamphlets. Kenkai not knowing how to read brings Velkhi over and the pamphlets read about how anyone can be a Chrysos Heir, asking for one sovereign coin. not having one they get annoyed that we are not rich enough. 

While Caledor and Yuichi are sparing a mysterious man pulled up and said “to those it concerns” and hands them a letter “folded summons”. it tells us to go to the West Stair. While heading there, we past by the market and finally notice a Statue to the Titan Kephale holding up a world that is huge. we make it to a door on the West Stair and notice another inorganic autonomous near the door, after a second we hand the note to it and it opens up the door.

we see two people in the room. one sitting on the stairs with a bident (Mydeimos) and one sitting in a chair (Aglaea). Aglaea gets up and starts to speak and wants us to recount our tales before getting here. she tells us that if we are suffering ill effects from the Mataxy to dunk our heads into the waters ahead of us. we learn that the Mataxy is another dimension that mortals are not suppose to be in for long (24 hours at most) lest the creatures inside get to them. Our quest is to go back to Janusopolis to look for one of the Chrysos Heirs, Castorice, and find out as much as you can. while we are talking to Aglaea, Mydeimos talks about how he is needed to deal with some weapons that were stolen from Castrum Kremnos.

Kephale, the world barer - one of the titans. held up the world as it was being created. 

Ione Kalyx - lieutenant of the skyfire wardens 
Mydeimos is taller than Yuichi by about 3-4 inches
Anaxagoras - one of the Chrysos Heirs
Castorice - the Chrysos Heir we are looking for. short pink hair wields a scythe
Charon - person in charge of the forge
