*Arc 1: Survivors of Janusopolis (Session 1-7)*

Selenodas, 7 Month of Joy, 4931 (Wevdas, 7 Phareon, 1E 808)
Velkhi got the MVP award.

we move on from this area to go to the next area where we see 4 warriors and an archer. however, these ones are petrified, we decide to move past of them. the next room Franzen finds a potato down here. down the corridor we find a pressure plate that at first Mayuri puts Pantera on but on second consideration realizes maybe we should check if there is a way out should Pantera be dispelled. noticing there is not, and thinking it is weight based Mayuri, Kenkai, and Yuichi decided to break a pillar off and place it on, noticing it did not budge. When placing a Balance Coin on it it did open the door, but Kenkai and Franzen were not so sure about that and decided to try and place more rocks. Yuichi then places his Balance Coin and the door opens.

after making it out of that shenanigans. we make it down to a place with pollenpump trees. Kenkai immediately grabs them and tries to eat it. Yuichi remembering these have to do with psychopomp and they are poisonous tells him not to eat it. everyone (except Mayuri) gets a couple pieces of this pollenpump. Caledor opens a box and finds a turtle and decides to keep him. a little further in we find a a journal and four soul coins. the journal seems familiar to Mayuri. during this time, one of the warriors moved. 

we make it to a waterfall where in the water we see Behir which are extremely violent when hungry. climbing up the ledge we find who we think we are looking for. Yuichi decides to try and jump over the river and fails going down the river. everyone else decides to go over the lilies that Velkhi produced. As we all approach, two Behir come out of the water hungry.
