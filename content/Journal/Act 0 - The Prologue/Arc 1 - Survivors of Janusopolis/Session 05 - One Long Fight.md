*Arc 1: Survivors of Janusopolis (Session 1-7)*

Selenodas, 7 Month of Joy, 4931 (Wevdas, 7 Phareon, 1E 808)
Mayuri got the MVP award.

after leaving the room with the Chrysos Heirs, we do some preparations and head for the gates to go back to Janusopolis. as we walk across the tops ruins we come across some skyfire wardens who gives us some trouble but presenting the folded summons quickly resends his troubles. he points us to Jossum, leader of their troope, who tells us that Castorice was last seen through here a couple days ago and has not returned, she likes to be near the soul trees down by the river of souls. he mentions there is many paths down but recommends the fastest path down as no matter which way you go there will be trouble. Titankin swarm the place and taken over Janusopolis. we realize that most of us cannot sneak our way through Sora and Yuichi decide to try and move on ahead but realize the futility of it and come back. we come to an opening where we see a couple of them 3 on the ground and 2 archers sitting up top. Yuichi climbs up, realizing he climbed up the wrong pillar assists sora with climbing up. when everyone gets into position, the fight starts. (this fight lasts for 2 hours)

Titankin - in the image of nikador, they are frozen in place
