*Arc 2: Roots of Janusopolis (Session 8-17)*

Astardas, 13 Phareon, 808 (Mnemodas, 13 Month of Joy, 4931)
Ulixes got the MVP 
Overcast, Humidity 62%, precipitation 47%, wind 5 m/s

Mayuri and Yuichi assault the Abyssla Anchor Nexus. After weakening the Nexus, Yuichi leaps over the ledge to assist Sir Caerwyn and Ulixes; Mayuri is knocked down as five Furiae fighters spawn. 

Zuzros, Delmore, Kenkai, Caledor, and Franzen team up to destroy the Abyssla Anchor Spire, weakening the Tether of Nyktos. 

Ulixes smashes a Furiae warrior that was pursuing Yuichi, but is downed by archers on the upper landing; Sir Caerwyn revives him with battle medicine. 

The party clears lesser Furiae spawns to concentrate on the Tether. Ulixes briefly emerges and blasts a monstrous plant with a ray of holy fire. Fighting continues until the team eliminates all Furiae spawns. 

With the field cleared, Sir Caerwyn lands a critical strike that deals massive damage, and the party launches a full-frontal assault on the Tether of Nyktos.
