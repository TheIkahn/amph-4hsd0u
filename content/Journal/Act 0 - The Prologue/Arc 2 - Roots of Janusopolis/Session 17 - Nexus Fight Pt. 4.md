Astardas, 13 Phareon, 808 (Mnemodas, 13 Month of Joy, 4931)
Ulixies got MVP
Overcast, Humidity 62%, precipitation 47%, wind 5 m/s

we continue our full on assault on the Tether of Nyktos. damaging the Tether enough caused it to spit out an Ichor of sorts. putting it to its limits, it went into a frenzy causing it to do more damage and also take more damage. Mayuri took the time to message the Chrysos Heirs that we were close to destroying the Tether to which Tribios told us that they will destroy their target within 12 seconds. we destroy our target 6 seconds after that. This causes the tether to Erupt into a green ball of flames and the roof to collapse. Franzen, Yuichi, Caledor, and Kenkai all get caught in this and go down. 

Yuichi breaks down when he gets up and realizes the last 3 days has been nothing but difficult for him and he believes its all his fault. Franzen embraces him and tells him its all alright and he doesn’t need to bare it all. during this time everyone introduces each other, except for Mayuri who knew the new people. 

Sir Caerwyn, Ulixes and Delmore gets a chance to shine for a moment. Sir Caerwyn is a Dullahan which is of Fae Origin. Ulixies and Caledor get into a talk about Philosophy. Delmore gets to use his exploding knife on Mayuri. 

While this is going on Mydeimos and Phainon enter in and notice that 4 more people have entered our ranks. they explain that the rest of the groups had trouble with their fights as well. they plan on having a feast later and everyone is invited to join.

as we are leaving Janusopolis, we see the Faerie warriors getting unpetrified they are becoming not hostile anymore.  

We level up.
