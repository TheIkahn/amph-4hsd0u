*Arc 3: Gathering Threads (Session 18-24)*

Selenodas, 21 Month of Joy, 4931 (Wevdas, 21 Phareon, 808)
Kenkai Got MVP
Overcast, Humidity 75%, precipitation 46%, wind 12 m/s (Golden Moon)

We started the session with combat, confusing everyone. Caledor discovered information pertaining to the events going on, noticing greyish motes of smoke surrounding both the enemies as well as Kenkai. As the enemies perish, they dissipate into smoke instead of dying normally. 

Sir Caerwyn dashed across the battlefield, cleaning up the archers; While this has been going on Yuichi, Kenkai, and Franzen were taking care of warriors and keeping the Praetor distracted. 

Delmore has been extending his links among the party, benefiting all with his potions. eventually he threw bottled lightning at the Philosopher and getting into the fray. 

After much wrestling with a warrior, both Caledor and Zuzros tag teamed a warrior taking it down; this resulted in just two enemies remaining on the battlefield. 

Franzen, Caledor, Yuichi, and Kenkai mobbed the Praetor; Yuichi tripped him repeatedly, and the rest took turns beating on him until Caledor did a critical on a spellstrike rending the Praetor into many parts. Delmore finished off the Philosopher with a death link. 

---- 
After the fight, we started to collectively feel sleepy and weary; we ended up waking up in the Grove's training grounds. We were approached by a stranger, explaining we were fighting memories of battles past. The stranger has introduced himself as a Sage of Helkolithism, Apollonides. Expressing interest in Caledor they offer attendance at classes too their school. 

When Apollonides left the group too their own whims they received a group message from Tribios inviting everyone too a speach in two days hosted by Aglaea. 

Franzen, Yuichi, Caerwyn, and Caledor, begins exploring the grove following a feeling of warmth. finding a giant golden butterfly with flying dryads around it. each person in it's presence perceiving different smells. Caerwyn attempts to touch the butterfly and it allows him and his horse too pass through it. Franzen attempts too touch it and the butterfly dissipates into thousands of small butterflies before entering something similar to a sanctuary. Yuichi and Caledor converse with one another as inside the butterfly Caerwyn and Franzen are havinf private discussions with Dryads. Yuichi and Caledor enter the butterfly and it feels natural too Yuichi while too Caledor it halts him in place and prevents him from entering. Yuichi turns back to try and help Caledor through. Trying to channel the feeling of love Caledor stumbles through into the butterfly with assistance. The place they've come too seems too bring peace and calm, some speaking to Dryads others exploring the area they've come too. The group ventures too the library on the lower floors of the area they've ventured. 

The next session will begin with Delmore in the Botany lab.
