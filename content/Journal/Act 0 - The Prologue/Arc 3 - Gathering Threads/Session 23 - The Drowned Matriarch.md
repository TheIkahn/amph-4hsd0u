*Arc 3: Gathering Threads (Session 18-24)*

Selenodas, 22 Month of Joy, 4931 (Wevdas, 22 Phareon, 808)
Franzen got MVP
Partly Cloudy, Humidity 64%, Precipitation 41%, Wind 9 m/s

We start this locked in combat with the Drowned Matriarch. its giant ligntning bolt comes down and hits nobody. when we break the shield it lets out a massive roar and 5 Behir start coming out of the woodworks. The Drowned Matriarch gears up and springs forward clawing different people along the way.

We end up eliminating the Drowned Matriarch and taking its guts. we notice there are eggs around the platform and eliminate them, Delmore gets upset that we destroy them. during this time meet a a Haniver as well. we decide to kill the rest of the behir in the cave. we kill 7 behirs and find 24 balance coins within 4 hours.

Kenkai, Delmore, Mayuri (Zuzros), Yuichi each get a boss part

We meet a Silvanshee, and we learn that it eats carrots from our garden. it can speak common as its from Nirvana. they are looking for a hero under the guise of a cat.

```
To Mayuri, Yuichi, Caledor, Kenkai, Franzen, Sir Caerwyn Kyrn Dreadmane, Delmore, Zuzros Rhiesis, and Ulixes of the Crucible,

I am sending this in writing because I would like you to read it before you decide what you want to do with the morning that follows.

Tomorrow at the second turn of Lucid I will speak to Okhema from the upper platform of Dawncloud. The address will not be brief and it will not be ordinary. What I am going to say has been overdue for a thousand years, and the city is going to need every steady voice it has standing visibly within while it lands.

You have earned the right to be on that platform. I would like you there.

Wear what Okhema's traditions ask of you. If you do not own such garments, my Garmentmakers will arrive at the Philoxenion at Sunrise and they will have the right cloth in the right hands one hour later. There is no debt incurred. Consider it a gift between neighbors.

If you would prefer not to attend, send the herald back with that answer and there will be no shadow on it. The work continues either way.

I hope we treat each other with honesty.

**Aglaea, Goldweaver, Heir of Mnestia, Dressmaster of Okhema**
```
