*Arc 3: Gathering Threads (Session 18-24)*

Mnemodas, 13 Month of Joy, 4931 (Astardas, 13 Phareon, 808)
Kenkai got MVP 

Sora and Velki have officially left the party now to travel their own path (Left the campaign) We go back to the Trilamp house (Caerwyn's thoughts: Peculiar structure, i wonder whom built it?) The party rest (Most long rest and return to full HP).  

- Zuzros, Caerwyn, and Kenkai tend to the Gardens 
- Delmore is confused about the state of this nations currency

Yuichi approaches Caerwyn and ask why he would travel with the party after a long winded conversation Caerwyn explains that Vireloth chose this land as their "Vacation" spot and that he is also here to establish a connection between this land and Elderglen Hollow. The two (and Delmore was also present) talk about Yuichi's homeland and about the Ritualistic fights with ancestors (Ghost?) to see if they are worthy of being reincarnated. 

We leave the Trilamp house and head closer to the world titans frozen frame and the inner city that lies near it. We head to the Court of Seasons and see Aglaea, Tribios, Mydeimos, Phainon. We are lead to Aglaea's weaving chambers (Where she makes boss weapons- Dressmaster's Quarters) Phainon provides the new members of the party with mystical slates (Phones). 

After deliberations many items were requested this day. 

Caerwyn ask Aglaea about how she acquired her powers. She knows not though she believes it stems from her bloodlines blessing from the titan Mnestia. 

After dinner in the Palace Aglaea leads the party away to present a Gift to us, She presents a new home to us, a previous shrine to Mnestia, it is called Philoxenion.

12 rooms (three unassigned)- nine can be private quarters - central atrium -training hall for full power fights - forge annex for crafting - Cool glass dome directly connected to light device of the statue - Teleslate booster (5g) - Private garden has fruit trees and 3x open plots for planting - sealed subceller for later discussion - 1x permanent staff member (8ft 4 Linnilae ,Yoten born). 

After settling in Delmore tries to convince Yuichi to give him some balance coins, Kenkai also makes a deal with and ENT to bring a boss soul in exchange for a ring of speech.
