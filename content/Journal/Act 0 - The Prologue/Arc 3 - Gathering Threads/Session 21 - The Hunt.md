*Arc 3: Gathering Threads (Session 18-24)*

Selenodas, 21 Month of Joy, 4931 - Eosdas, 22 Month of Joy, 4931 (Wevdas, 21 Phareon, 808 - Mortdas, 22 Phareon, 808)
Franzen Got MVP
Overcast, Humidity 41%, Precipitation 7%, Wind 9 m/s

Sir Caerwyn is gathering up folks to hunt an alpha Behir which is currently halting efforts of restoring Janusopolis also killing refugees, he recruits Delmore and Zuzros. Delmore and Yuichi begin talking as Delmore holds a water sac filled with Void Shrooms Yuichi is appalled by this. Yuichi wants Delmore to promise to him that he won’t be a liability to the group at hand. Yuichi walks away from the conversation and tells Franzen and Sir Caerwyn that Delmore is going to kill the entire group. Sir Caerwyn tries to recruit Yuichi but he is hesitant after his last adventures around Behir. Sir Caerwyn and Yuichi go back and forth about what it means to go after something. Yuichi believes that Skyfire Wardens can handle it. Mayuri suddenly appears behind Sir Caerwyn. Caledor agrees to go along. After mentioning to the group that they are very territorial and killing people, Yuichi changes his mind. 

We learn that Behir are an invasive species to Amphoreus and thus brand new so no books have been written on them. Mayuri begrudgingly agrees and the party sets off to prepare to fight this Alpha Behir.

- - - 
Eosdas, 22 Month of Joy, 4931 (Mortdas, 22 Phareon, 808)
Partly Cloudy, Humidity 49%, Percipitation 8%, Wind 6 m/s

Kenkai receives a Mnemonic Seed from Franzen and it allows Kenkai to read as he is viewing the memories of others who can read, but only while he is holding this seed. We head out towards Janusopolis. While getting there we see some Caligni in our path while attempting to stealth passed them Franzen Trips down a hill and falls in front of them. due to language barriers none of us can talk to the Caligni, Kenkai casts Translate which allows us to talk to them. we learn one of them is 4th in line to the boss of the Amphoreus sect of the Caligni. They had no idea what a behir is but they knew there was a ton of loot on the inside of the cave. the party does not intervene in their affairs and allows them to continue. the party questions if they just sentenced a bunch of people to die. we decide to press forward. 

The Behir made quick work of the Caligni, and when we tried to approach the Behir it scurred off. we learn that Behir hunt in packs they prefer numbers and will scurry away if by themselves. 

Caligni are not native to Amphoreus they are bandits and thieves that follow the forsaken. 

```
**Entry One.**

Squad Kite Six, on detached scout duty by order of the Wing. Mapping assignment. Myself, Kallias, Brasidas, Timon, Phyle. Objective is the flooded cavern system below the new strata, suspected den of the beasts harrying the southern recovery teams. We are to chart it, count them, and mark the big one if it shows itself. We are not to engage. I have repeated this to Brasidas three times and will likely repeat it a fourth.

Entered through the cleft at first bell. Air stinks of storms. There is no storm down here. Phyle says the smell is the beasts themselves, that their hide carries it. Charted the entry corridor and the first pools. The water is deeper than it looks everywhere. We sound it with poles before every crossing now, after Timon went in to his chest on what looked like an ankle puddle.

Marked the long gallery of columns ahead for tomorrow. Made camp on the dry shelf inside the cleft. First watch is mine.

**Entry Two.**

The gallery is bad ground. Still water the whole length of it, columns thick as old trees, and the ceiling is alive. We never saw one move. We saw where they had been. Fresh gouges in the stone overhead, big as a plough's cut, and a shed length of hide draped over a column like a banner someone forgot. Blue-white, near as long as our boat back home. Brasidas wanted to take it for proof. It is heavier than it looks. We left it.

Counted what we could from cover. Six distinct, by size and scarring, between the gallery and the pool chamber beyond it. There will be more below the water. There are always more below the water.

The pool chamber to the east is fed by a silver thread off the great river. A soultree grows over it, of all things, crooked as a beggar's finger. Strange to find something gentle down here. We did not linger. The water in that one is deep past sounding and none of us liked how quiet it was.

**Entry Three.**

Heard the big one today. Did not see it. Hear is the wrong word. Phyle's teeth ached first, then mine, then the water in the gallery started to shiver in rings with no wind and no footfall. It went on for a ten-count and stopped. Within a hundred breaths there were three of the beasts moving through the gallery below us, fast, all the same direction, like hounds called to a whistle.

It sings to them. That is the only way I can put it. The big one sings and they come.

Kallias found a way up the broken shelves in the shaft chamber, says there is a route across the top if your arms hold. From the high ledge she swears she could see a glow off the far cathedral chamber, low and pale, in a side hollow. Eggs, she thinks. We head to where the Behir ran off to, and find a strange note on the floor, Franzen goes to pick it up and two Behir ambush us. We take them out with Relative ease. Franzen and Yuichi get a scuffle. Yuichi believes that we need to play it absolutely safe and Franzen is trying to reassure him that we are playing it safe right now. Yuichi then berates Franzens actions and strikes to his core about his ability to protect Kenkai. Franzen snaps yells “outside” and walks away. Mayuri tries to reason with Yuichi and then realizes she is going nowhere and attempts to find Franzen. Delmore then tries to communicate with Yuichi and is able to make some progress. 
```

Yuichi attempts to go apologize to Franzen for being a coward. Franzen then bares his chest to Yuichi and shows his scars to him. They then get into another heated argument. Mayuri then chimes in and tells Yuichi why he left Kahnmae Forest, which he was banished from, and shares Franzen 11 years of brutal endurance by his mother. When Franzen asks how she would know this, she says that she’s known him most of his life. Franzen then says he is no condition to fight right now as he has a lot to think about. although Sir Caerwyn and Yuichi want to continue on but Mayuri and Franzen say we need to rest.
