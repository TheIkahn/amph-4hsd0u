*Arc 4: Janus’s Coreflame (Session 25-32)*

Pyrodas, 25 Month of Joy, 4931 
Ulixes got MVP 
Who the fuck cares about weather 

The party decided to keep fighting the Praetors, kinda bumrushing them. First was an Electricity-based Elite, which was destroyed with relative ease by the party. Yuichi would have went down to its chain lightning attacks, but Zuzro's used Martyr's Intervention and ate the excess damage for Yuichi. 

The Elites have a pattern - at least the Elemental ones. They have 50% attunement, infusion, and absorption of that sword, equaling 75% bonus strike damage of their element, and boosted elemental damage. These Praetor's were corrupted, therefore gaining even more typed infusion when they unpetrified. Each Praetor was doing 150% bonus damage of their respective element. 

After the Electricity one fell, then came the Cold one. It was dispatched relatively easily considering the party learned how to Trip the Praetor, putting it on its ass. Mayuri used her Hour to perma slow and make it susceptible to energy damage. Yuichi tried to buff his weapon and got actually one shot because of a crit that dealt like 135 damage to Yuichi through his resists. Get fucked nerd, Interact is a Manipulate. Ulixes healed em, then they beat the creature. No loot from dis one (electricity catalyst from the electricity one) Afterwards, came the SCARY one. Sanguine! BLEED! MURDER! Except the party got smacked by it once after Franzen took an insta down hit for Kenkai, then they learned how to trip. Remember, Praetors do not know what to do when you trip them. They lost immediately when they got tripped every time. 

Afterwards, motherfucking Death came knocking at their door so they ran out the place. They went to go back to Okhema, made it back after a timeskip, and then my fucking shop got deleted before anyone could buy anything, but thankfully Claude is a G and got it back for me.
