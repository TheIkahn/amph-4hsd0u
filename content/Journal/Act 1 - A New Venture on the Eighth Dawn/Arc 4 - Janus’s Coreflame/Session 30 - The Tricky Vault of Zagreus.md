*Arc 4: Janus’s Coreflame (Session 25-32)*

Pyrodas, 25 Month of Joy, 4931 (Middas, 25 Phareon, 808)
Sir Carewyn Kyrn got MVP
Partly Cloudy, Humidity 73%, Precipitation 28%, Wind 11 m/s

the fight continues on. steelwarden 1 goes down to a trip and an ancestral blast. steelwarden 2 get crit failure on by lightningstrike and loses a round. we easily dispatch of steel warden 3 and then take forever to kill steel warden 4. after the fight Yuichi wanted to climb up the pillars however they were too tall and had smooth edges so he slid back down. Mayuri used fly and noticed a chest up there and got a elemental catalyst (lesser, water) and flawed tide gem. 

We walk into the next room and see a vault door. after a bit of investigation we learn that something can light up the center. after further more investigation we see the lantern can light it up as it has the power of a titan. putting the lantern out we see a sigil pop out of ground. its the sigil of Zagreus. we connect it to the hand of Zagreus and bring it in here, making sure to go in groups of two to make sure we don’t activate the gargoyles. after a bit of thinking we use the hand to punch the vault door to open it up. the vault looks empty and when we go in our suspicion is confirmed as it is indeed cleaned out. Two Greater Herexen of Zagerus come out and start a fight with us. 

The greater Herexen of Zagerus start off by giving Sir Carewyn a curse: Coin of whimsy. people can take it by insulting the person cursed by it. they also have the ability to make people go the wrong direction. we kill the first one and it explodes in a harm spell. after some moving around the second one also dies and explodes into a harm spell.
