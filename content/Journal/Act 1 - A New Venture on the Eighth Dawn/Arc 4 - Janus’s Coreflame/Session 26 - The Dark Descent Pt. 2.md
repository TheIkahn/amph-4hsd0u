*Arc 4: Janus’s Coreflame (Session 25-32)*

Pyrodas, 25 Month of Joy, 4931
Ulixies got MVP
Partly Cloudy, Humidity 87%, Precipitation 30%, Wind 14 m/s

After dispatching the group we encountered. We walk a distance and find a broken bridge. after some deliberation, we look to see how we can get across, most can but some cannot. Kenkai takes a gamble and goes into the darkness to find another way. Taking a bit of damage from the darkness Kenkai does find another way however there are some rocks covering it. deciding that way to be better as the distance would be shorter we head that path. We start moving the rocks but Sir Carewyn is a bit distracted and has rocks fall on Kenkai and Yuichi. with the combined strength of Kenkai, Yuichi and Mayuri they are able to clear the rocks. Kenkai then gets onto the other bridge and then lowers a rope down for the rest of us to cross. the rest of the party follows suit.

We continue and then turn a corner bright lights come out. Tribios gasps as we finally see the Temple of the Three Fates. There is a bright energy coming from it that is radiating towards the party. The temple use to be a political center for Janusopolis. Kenkai and Sir Carewyn notice a small piece of red fabric on the ground (abyss-tattered red cloth)

>A torn length of heavy cloth, deep red going almost black at the folds, its edge-band woven in a repeating diamond twill that catches the light like scales. It was working clothes once: hard-worn, sun-faded, mended many times over by the same patient hand, sized for a big man. The mending is good. Whoever owned this fixed his own things, and had for years.
>
>One entire edge is scorched in a way fire does not scorch. The fibers are not burned so much as unmade, fused into brittle dark glass where something touched them.

Phainon walks up looks at the piece and mutters under his breath “the black tide”. we then scale up and get into the Central Chamber. in the walls there is a bunch of sigils such as birds. then one that looks like Tribios Core-flame. which Tribios confirms that is Janus Vault. Phainon then is able to light up the Braziers in the room, and a star is in the center. Tribios says its the guiding star as Janus was the first titan. Tribios is quite nervous about this whole ordeal. 

as we get going through the ordeal a figure comes down from the ceiling slowly decending. Zuzros notices that Phainon blade is reacting to the man. This figure is a dawnspeaker serving Kephale. Kephale willingly gave up his core flame. the price 12 times over, every flame is price, and the titan paid the price. Mayuri questions the man if they should just sit there and die. he has been here for 400 years, has not seen. Kenkai and Mayuri try to reason with him diplomatically and intimidating him. none succeed and he then holds up a light that dazzles everyone except Phainon, Yuichi, Kenkai and Mayuri. he then holds up the light and asks the creatures of the night to come forth. 

Zuzuros was able to bring Metranoros to the ground with earthbinding.
Zuzuros disarms Metranoros. He lets out a “no” as it falls to the ground. time slows down for a second as the energy being channeled, Zuzros then kicks it away and at that second the amount of energy contained inside of it explodes killing all the Furiae warriors that were in the vicinity and incapacitating Metranoros. However, another creature appears from the darkness, an abyss Beast.

Metranoros is only weak to franzen bonotelli 
the lamp he is holding up is burning very quickly
he reduces non-melee damage by 75% and takes normal from melee.
he has 400 ft of aura that gets reduced for every stack he’s missing and can target anyone inside of his radius. 
reflex is his worst save
since he is a creature who bears kephales domain: stored light, carried weight, counted days. Its current Worldbearing determines the strength of its marked abilities.
