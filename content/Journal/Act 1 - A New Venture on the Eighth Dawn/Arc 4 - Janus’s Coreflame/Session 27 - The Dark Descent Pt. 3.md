*Arc 4: Janus’s Coreflame (Session 25-32)*

Middas, 25 Phareon, 808 (Pyrodas, 25 Month of Joy, 4931)
Zuzros got MVP
Partly Cloudy, Humidity 87%, Precipitation 30%, Wind 14 m/s

We are pounced on by a large evernight beast, yuichi quickly grabs the lantern disarmed from the dawnspeaker and rushes it to the brazier, caledor takes it from him and lights it all while the party members heal themselves to fight the creature. The light from the brazier shines fighting off the evernight from tormenting the party. They then work together with Tribios who regains herself, and they all slay the evernight beast after Tribios uses her ultimate to obliterate it with a critical failure. **Kenkai talks to Metanoros, and convinces him to "stop it, go get help" and he starts walking to Okhema.** Afterward, tribios opens a door south of the room and enters, inside, her coreflame awaits. Upon taking it everyones head fill with cryptic messages kenkai swiftly casts a spell to allow everyone to understand. 

**Janus: OVERTHROW THE GODS - RETURN THE COREFLAMES 
Janus: INHERIT DIVINE AUTHORITY - FORGE GODLY MIRACLES 
Janus: GO KILL - GO MOURN 
Janus: KILL MY KIN - MOURN OUR FATE 
Janus: FERRY THE SPIRITS OF THE GODS - NOURISH THE LAND 
WITH DIVINE ESSENCE 
Janus: YET, THOU SHALT REMEMBER - THOU MUST REMEMBER 
Janus: ALL SHALL BID FAREWELL TO ONE, AND THAT PERSON ALONE WILL WITNESS THE MIRACLE 
Janus: SUCH... IS DESTINY 
Janus: ...** 

The messages tell us to kill the 'gods' presumably the titans, and nourish the land with their blood. Janus the Titan dies. Afterwards, Tribios falls unconscious after crying out to her mother in anguish, and a strange doorway opens under golden blood, we travel through as phainon takes care to tribios. We're met with a large colosseum-like area filled with doors. Two of which find of note, one thats chained and locked and another leading to a labyrinth with enemies inside. The voice behind the chain door demands bones, only then will it allow us past. We then gather and decide to explore the labyrinth for now, Carewyn quickly meets with phainon to let him know we will be a minute to explore, he says he will mark the way we came in, that way we may leave easily through the metaxy. Phainon and Tribios leave. Delmore and Sir Caerwyn talk to a creature called "The Weightmaster" with traits of Law and Celestial. The party sees hostiles, and level up, regaining their mythic points, and starting to head down into the dungeon to seek bones. 

Phainon also warned the party about the third Fate titan perhaps being within the confines of this temple.
