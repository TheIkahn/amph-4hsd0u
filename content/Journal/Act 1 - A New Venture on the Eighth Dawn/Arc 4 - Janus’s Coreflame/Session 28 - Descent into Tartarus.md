*Arc 4: Janus’s Coreflame (Session 25-32)*

Middas, 25 Phareon, 808 (Pyrodas, 25 Month of Joy, 4931)
Kenkai Ventura got MVP
Partly Cloudy, Humidity 67%, Precipitation 33%, Wind 8 m/s

skill points were divied out to Sir Caerwyn, Ulixies, Delmore and Zuzros. After a bit of deciding we try to stealth our way through the next encounter. However, due to the party’s poor stealth skill, we have to initiate combat. there are Three Trespassers. 

After the combat was over Kenkai noticed a tablet on the ground picking it up. It looks like an item that attunes specifically to Mayuri. After giving it to Mayuri, she attunes to it and now has three geometric shapes floating around her and they attune to her witchwarping skills, this item is called “_Votive Three: The Quiet Chorus_”. Beyond this there is a lift we dont know which way it goes. Mayuri decides to fly up and notices a chest in it contains a Talant and a Freezing Gladius and some other stuff.

**Trespassers**
they do not have any vulnerabilities
Their resistances to all damage 12 (except force, spirit, imaginary, anima, ghost touch, or oneiric. double resistance vs non-magical). 
warded 35
They are immune to disease, petrification, bleed
traits: quantum, spirit
weakest save fortitude
they have a phase two: which makes them regain all their hp when they go down, they lose the ability to do after image but now can do collapse point and amplified output
they also explode after they die in a 15-foot emanation. they can do this without triggering the reaction
they are also permanently concealed
