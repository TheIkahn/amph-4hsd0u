*Arc 6 (Session 38-)*

Eosdas, 1 Month of Everday, 4931 
Yuichi got MVP 
Partly Cloudy, Humidity 75%, Precipitation 30%, Wind 8 m/s

Downtime events happened.

We flash back to 2 days ago: Kenkai and Franzen are tearing up the garden, Zuzros, who has been tending to the garden for the past week, walks in to see the mess. Kenkai explains that the garden was in a terrible state and needed to be replanted properly, also giving him chance to plant new things in the garden. Kenkai apologizes for ripping it up without warning. Stonemelon, Brocolli, Carrots, Onions, etc. Kenkai and Zuzros get into a squabble about what the difference between a god and a titan. Zuzros corrects some knowledge to Kenkai about Terravox being the chrysos heir to Georios. 

back to the present, Carewyn and Yuichi went out to the temple to add our camps there in advance so we can descend down Tartarus again. the rest of us are getting up to get there. Kenkai finds a blood orange tea with honeydew in the garden not sure whose it is, he just ends up drinking it. It was a pleasant drink.

Heading over to the temple of three fates we set down our tents talking over placements. we talk a bit (we look through Kenkai’s ‘spicy’ books) and then Delmore offers glowrods to Caledor, Yuichi and Franzen. 

We head into Tarturus and go right this time. we encounter some Hound of Tindalos. behind a strange black wall 

They have mitigation to Temporal damage allowing them to heal.

Yuichi finds a door. there is a sequence of 5 doors. kenkai then slams the doors now yuichi thinks that door slammed on their own. Mayuri starts opening doors, and then says this might be the act of Zagreus upon saying his name his symbol appears above the door. 

after a couple minutes we decide to go through that black wall and see theres more to the room. we investigate further find another door. Mayuri goes through and see its an empty room with two objects inside of it: Whismy-Bone Astragalos and Corrupted Lesser Insight. with nothing else we go back into the room and go up stairs in the back go down those stairs and run into another black wall that we cannot pass further.
