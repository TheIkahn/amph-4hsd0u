*Arc 5: The Ring of Sigils (Session 33-37)*

Middas, 27 Phareon, 808 (Pyrodas, 27 Month of Joy, 4931)
Franzen got MVP 
Who the fuck cares about weather 

Kenkai and Franzen start their day by having a wholesome moment where Kenkai presents a new shield as a gift to Franzen. Then, The party mainly continues shopping for a while. Since Aglaea didn't have any information yet on the next flamechase, nor anything that could help locate the person in Amphoreus tied to Yuichi, Zuzros and Yuichi leave to meet with Phainon and Mydeimos, who are relaxing in the indoor springs. Zuzros informs phainon about his vision that Terravox is on his way back to Okhema. Phainon is pleased to hear this, and suggests a welcoming party is in order. Yuichi reminds the two of them that they are ready for the next flamechase, and that their party is ready for the call anytime, they respond by telling the two of them Mydeimos will be traveling with them next time. Yuichi also requests Mydeimos for further training, he agrees, participating party members are to meet with him tomorrow, afterwards Zuzros and Yuichi head home. Meanwhile, Kenkai walks to the graveyard outskirts alone in search of the Kephale worshipping man they'd met in the three fates palace. He did not find him, instead, he comes across a bear-sized oozing living sack of critters and creatures. He instantly gets on his phone and messages his party about it. Yuichi identifies the thing as a creature made by someone evil, the creatures are suffering, it would be best to end its magic. After some finagling, Zuzros manages to get within line of sight of kenkai, despite their distance, his ability allows him to cast dispel magic through their gap, and cleanse the creature of its magic, the creatures all scurry off, and the two feel a sense of pride of helping a heap of tormented animals. The party agrees to follow the ring of sigils they found tomorrow after training with Mydeimos.
