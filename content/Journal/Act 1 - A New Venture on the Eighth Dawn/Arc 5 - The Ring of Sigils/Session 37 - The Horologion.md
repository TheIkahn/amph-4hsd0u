*Arc 5: The Ring of Sigils (Session 33-37)*

Selenodas, 28 Month of Joy, 4931 
Caledor got MVP 
Partly Cloudy, Humidity 85%, Precipitation 36%, Wind 10 m/s

Yuichi takes the lantern for a second and goes up the wall and finds a chest with some coins in it. Kenkai comments on Caledor’s Gladius. we then take the time to set up a camp inside of this place. 

jobs at the camp:
Delmore - Cooking
Yuichi - Scouting
Sir Carewyn - Guarding
Zuzros - Tending 
Caledor - Research 
Mayuri - Research 

Delmore begins by cooking he makes a half keen half steady meal. Yuichi begins scouting and sees a titankin in the next room. Delmore tries to sit down and collapses a clock falling down 25ft and he finds a skull and a Balance Coin. Mayuri learns that there is Abyssal creatures here and titankin near the temple of the three fates. deep in the crevices there is voidsteel that can be mined and extracted. Voidsteel is a very powerful metal that would be hard to get to in the parties current state. Caledor is able to do his fable study and crits getting 2 research points. Mayuri and Caledor then decide to look at Mayuri journal however nothing new comes up in it. 

after resting we go to the Horologio Clock where Mayuri and Xander talk about the Theology and their perpetuity. Zuzros explains as well that the titans are in a state of aliveness however they are in different states of corruptness. while in that conversation we notice that titankin that Yuichi was talking about early. it however says nothing and looks at us, Kenkai attempts to talk to it. Yuichi holds the ring up to the creature and it casts seen the unseen and then flies away. 

We go up to this pedestal which contains a chest. while we are hanging around the seen the unseen. 

Caledor invests in the ring and the puts his hand near the sigil and theres a blue flash of light and all the clocks in the tower stop ticking and water starts flooding into the pedestal. an arcane beam then shoots up towards the clock. the clock looks like its reflectable surface. yuichi goes up to the clock and starts spinning it Kenkai hears a click at 10 o’clock then he turns it again at 4 o’clock hears another click. going clockwise it at 2 o’clock the clock clicks again and the building shakes an object falls out. This object is Black Glass Disc 

>A disc of black glass about the width of a palm, set in a bronze collar of a different, newer alloy than the dial it was found in. The collar is ringed with fine runes in no script you know, more like a list of measurements with the units left off than like writing. The glass is cold, and it stays cold in a warm hand. It reflects nothing: not a face, not a lamp, not the sky.
>Found at the centre of the great hour-dial in the Horologion beside the Temple of the Three Fates, where the Ring of Sigils led.

We notice the disc has some sort of runes that are written similar to how caledor writes his own notes. however these ones are not known to us. left with nothing else as the ring pointed to the disc. we decide to head back to Okhema.
