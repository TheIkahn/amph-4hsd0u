*Arc 5: The Ring of Sigils (Session 33-37)*

Pyrodas, 28 Month of Joy, 4931 (Middas, 28 Phareon, 808)
Franzen got MVP 
it is weather outside

We continue our bout of combat. Tobari continues to keep his distance during the fight and makes multiple flurry of blows attacks with his sword to varied success. for the most part though he does manage to keep the molting's the Seam-Mother leaves from ripening and spawning her eldritch brood. As a party we manage to kill off all the Seam-Mothers underlings / spawn and begin to focus her down. As we manage to form a battle line with Caerwyn and Tobari on one side the Seam-mother is flanked from the opposite side by Yuichi, Franzen and Kenkai. We seemed to have the upper hand until the Seam-Mother split herself along her abdomen to perform an unholy "Birth" of an Abyss beast that is flung out over the heads of the three where it quicky establishes itself as a viable threat from its flanking position. After this "birth" The Seam-Mother decides to close the distance on the isolates Caldor. Kenkai falls back to flank the abyss beast with Yuichi. Franzen, Tobari, and Caerwyn close to within striking distance of the seam-mother once more. while manuvering into the new position Tobari manages to land two devastating hits on the Abyss beast. 

Around this time Caledor proves that he is not as "trapped" as the Seam-Mother belives, Summoning a wall of fire upon it diverting its attention back to Franzen and the once again closing in Yuichi and Kenkai. Delmore fires a shot from around the fallen statue he finds himself behind then has a malfunction in his invention. After another while the Mother has its attention elsewhere Caledor lands a spell strike to critically devastating effect. (Current highest Dmg roll in any campaign)

Then before it can recover the party lands more attacks and Kenkai manages to land the killing blow splitting the Seam-Mother along the abdomen where it had given "birth" not long before showering himself and Franzen in her guts and viscera as she collapsed defeated. 

After the arduous fight the batterd party turned to address Tobari, Yuichi and Kenkai did most of the talking with and interjection from Franzen. Ultimately from conversing we find that Tobari is an incredibly old/Powerful Kitsune whom also exiled from Yuichi's homeland. He arrived thru some gateway in search of the flame chase not to dissimilar to our own predicament. After gently but firmly declining to join us, we provide directions to Okhema and that he could utilize the Metaxi to shorten his journey. After parting ways the party closes in on the rings pinged location which is a cluster of three twisted elven buildings. we manage to climb thru the second story window of the pinged building. we do this to avoid the Stelewardens flanking either side of the main door. inside we find a central courtyard like room that we decide would be perfect to setup a much needed campsite in.
