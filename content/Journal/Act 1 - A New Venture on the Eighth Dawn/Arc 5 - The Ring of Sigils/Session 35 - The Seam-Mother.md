*Arc 5: The Ring of Sigils (Session 33-37)*

Middas, 28 Phareon, 808 (Pyrodas, 28 Month of Joy, 4931)
Zuzros got MVP 
Partly Cloudy, Humidity 75%, Precipitation 38%, Wind 13 m/s

We pick up in the middle of combat. mysterious man (Tobari) comes in and helps the party with the skitterlings. we hear the ground shaking under our feet after a bit. we then hear a ear piercing skreech and a Abyssal Seam-Mother comes out and splits the earth immediately causing some damage to people nearby, its using earthly substances to protect itself. upon further inspection we notice that the mysterious man is constantly healing up the damage of the ever night as if the moment he gets torn apart it gets healed up over. We cleared up most of the skitterlings and now have just the Abyssal Seam-Mother.
