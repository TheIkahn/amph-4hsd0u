*Arc 5: The Ring of Sigils (Session 33-37)*

Selenodas, 28 Month of Joy, 4931 (Wevdas, 28 Phareon, 808)
No MVP, No homework last session
Partly Cloudy, Humidity 84%, Precipitation 26%, Wind 14 m/s

We pick up this session learning about the new Astrean Command Center (Thanks Hayden). We decided we are going to figure out what the ring we got way back when we found Castorice and then go a bit further. there are behir nearby. while walking through we manage to bump into 4 behirs and begin a fight. one of the behirs move up towards us and hits kenkai. Zuzros moves up to a different behir and shielding taunts 3 of the behir. the behir in front of Zuzros hits him and then grabs Zuzros and swallows him. Sir Caerwyn then tries to recall knowledge and then hits the behir twice. Caledor hastes himself and Yuichi then strolls in. Franzen uses the Doorwarden Stance then moves up to the behir that hit Kenkai. Mayuri enlarges her Quantum field then uses the votive three: the door to immobilize the behir that swallowed Zuzoros for 1 minute. Kenkai then runs up to the behir that hit him flanking with Franzen and strikes the behir. a third behir strolls up and misses on Caledor. Delmore overdrives, then draws and uses blood bomb. yuichi sudden charges and gets behind the behir that swallowed Zuzros, then strikes with his spear getting it to spit him out. the behir does a claw storm and hits franzen, delmore and mayuri. Zuzoros taunts and then does a transfer vitality to kenkai. the behir tries a claw storm but Yuichi does a standstill and kills it before it can do it. Sir Caerwyn recalls knowledge then reloads and shoots another shot. Caledor Spiras and does a ton of damage. Franzen raises his shield then instructive strikes the behir he was fighting. mayuri anchoring strikes with the antiphon and has pantera stroll up at the same time, then with a tandom boost pantera overwhelming combinations with a fist and enchanted rock to kill the behir. caledor was able to dispatch of the third behir. delmore pulls out whisperbroe and uses ooze ammunition and causes immobilization. 

After combat, mayuri points out that she triangulated the ring compass and surmised that its a 90 mile hike. a little defeated by the long hike we go back to okhema to resupply and then take the metaxy. we take the one thats next to the janusopolis temple as its the closest. While walking down we are disturbed by some skitterlings and abyss beast. during the middle of the fight a person in the distance closes in. this is the person that Mayuri felt their presence and who Yuichi was looking for.
