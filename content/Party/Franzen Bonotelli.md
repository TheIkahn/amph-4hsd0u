## 🎯 Personal Quests

> [!note] Priest of the Embraced
> Franzen is a Priest of the Order of the Embraced, who serve Milani, the Everbloom. A Priest's work is teaching: spreading the Order's word and offering a place in it to those he finds worthy. Bringing three into the Order raises a Priest to Deacon. A priest of the Order once taught him that love can protect rather than wound, and he means to teach it in turn. He brought a couple of people into the Order on the road but never made it back to teach them, because Kenkai still needed him.
>
> - [ ] Bring enough new members into the Order to rise to Deacon.
> - [ ] Teach someone that love can protect rather than wound.
