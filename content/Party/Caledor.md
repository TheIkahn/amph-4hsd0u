## 🎯 Personal Quests

> [!note] The Call
> Back in Lothern, Caledor began to feel a call beyond his family's walls, pulling him toward a place he didn't know: Amphoreus. He couldn't explain it, only that it felt meant for him. He left with his blade, his magic and the memory of the father who believed in him, hoping Amphoreus would give him a purpose and a place where he isn't ashamed of who he is. On the way he met Yuichi, who chose him as a rival. Since then Phainon has beaten him at arm-wrestling.
>
> - [ ] Find what called you to Amphoreus.
> - [ ] Get the edge on Yuichi.
> - [ ] Get your rematch with Phainon.

> [!note] The Closed Garden
> **Location:** The Grove of Epiphany
>
> The Grove keeps what it knows for its members. The archives, the real tutors, the crafting halls and the members' market sit behind a formal induction, which takes a sponsor among the Grove's members, demonstrated worth, and a ceremony. Members then earn standing through study and service, and one with enough standing may petition to found a sanctioned satellite school under the Grove's name. For Caledor that means a school of sword and sorcery, which Okhema doesn't have. As he was leaving, Sage Apollonides came to find him, which isn't how it usually goes. Induction requires a sponsor, the Sage said, and nothing more.
>
> - [x] Learn how the Grove admits its own.
> - [ ] Win a sponsor.
> - [ ] Demonstrate your worth.
> - [ ] Be inducted.
> - [ ] Earn standing through study and service.
> - [ ] Petition to found a sanctioned satellite school.

> [!note] The Opposition
> **Location:** Okhema
>
> At Dawncloud, Caledor found Caenis stewing in a corner: a half-giant who believes Aglaea cares for the Heirs and not Okhema's people, and that when the Black Tide comes Aglaea will flee onto the Flame-Chase and leave the city to drown. She means to stop her, and says it will take some bureaucracy. They traded Teleslates. When she rose to object during the address, she caught Caledor's eye and sat back down.
>
> - [x] Hear Caenis out.
> - [x] Exchange Teleslates with her.
> - [ ] Learn what Caenis means to do about Aglaea.
