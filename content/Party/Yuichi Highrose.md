## 🎯 Personal Quests

> [!note] Undefeated
> In Kahnmae Forest, Yuichi never once lost a bout at the Lifeway, and the elders barred him from fighting for it. He left with his mother's blessing and her portrait to find the toughest creatures he could. On the road he met Caledor and chose him as his rival. After the Tether he apologised to Franzen and the party; Franzen said trust would take time, and Mayuri said it takes more than words.
>
> - [ ] Become the strongest.
> - [ ] Get the edge on Caledor.
> - [ ] Earn back the party's trust.

> [!note] Someone From Home
> At Dawncloud, Mayuri felt someone deeply tied to Yuichi arrive in Amphoreus. When she told him two days later, he said he couldn't focus on that yet. Back in Okhema after the descent, Aglaea had nothing that could help find them.
>
> - [x] Ask Aglaea for help finding them.
> - [ ] Find out who has come to Amphoreus, and why. *Journal: it was [[Tobari]], met in Sessions 34–36.*
