## 🎯 Personal Quests

> [!note] Georios's Word
> Zuzros found his purpose the day he saw Terravox, Georios's chosen, passing through. Since then he has carried the word of Georios and Terravox from settlement to settlement, working the fields, treating the wounded and standing guard for whoever needs it. When his power woke, as he healed a critter he'd taken a beating to protect, he knew what it was for: healing and protection for those who deserve it, with himself at the front. The farming families outside Okhema's walls remember him, and he's been back to work their fields and bless their irrigation channels.
>
> - [ ] Carry the word of Georios and Terravox through Okhema.
> - [ ] Heal and protect those who deserve it.
> - [ ] Keep faith with the farmsteads outside Okhema's walls.

> [!note] Terravox Returns
> **Location:** Okhema
>
> At the well beneath the Temple, Zuzros prayed and gave his holy symbol of Georios to the flame, and Georios answered: Terravox is making his way back to Okhema, flying on Dromas across a desert, possibly days away, and it isn't looking good. Back in Okhema, Zuzros told Phainon, who was glad and said a welcoming party is in order.
>
> - [x] Seek Georios's word at the well.
> - [x] Tell Phainon that Terravox is coming.
> - [ ] Be in Okhema when Terravox arrives.
> - [ ] Throw Terravox a welcoming party.
> - [ ] Learn what has gone wrong.
