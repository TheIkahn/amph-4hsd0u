## 🎯 Personal Quests

> [!note] The Golden City
> In a tavern, Mayuri overheard older travellers talking about a golden city hidden far beyond the known roads that shimmers in and out of reach. Some had glimpsed it in dreams; others swore the gods hid it. It gives whoever reaches it what they most desire, and nobody who sets out for it comes back. Mayuri set out to find it anyway.
>
> - [ ] Learn whether anyone in Amphoreus has heard of the Golden City.
> - [ ] Find the Golden City.

> [!note] Mayuri's Codex
> **Location:** Okhema
>
> The codex had always been closed to her. At Dawncloud, with Castorice beside her, it opened for the first time and showed a private conversation between Castorice and Aglaea, about Aglaea. Aglaea read it herself and judged it a century old. She asked Mayuri to bring her any new conversation that surfaces, and said it may be shown to the other Chrysos Heirs. That same night Castorice took Mayuri's hand. Anyone who touches Castorice dies, and Mayuri did not.
>
> - [x] Decipher the codex for the first time.
> - [x] Show the codex to Aglaea.
> - [ ] Bring Aglaea the next conversation that surfaces.
> - [ ] Learn why the codex shows you what it shows.
> - [ ] Learn why Castorice's touch spared you.

> [!note] Two Doors in Okhema
> **Location:** Okhema
>
> Two names kept coming up after the address. The **Garmentmaker's Collegium**, Aglaea's own artisan school, whose weaving goes far deeper than clothing; you get in by apprenticeship or the Dressmaster's invitation. And the **Brass Orthograph**, a clockwork workshop in the market district whose keeper has taken a student on rare occasions, though nobody can name the last one.
>
> - [ ] Seek a place at the Garmentmaker's Collegium.
> - [ ] Learn what the Brass Orthograph's keeper teaches, and to whom.
