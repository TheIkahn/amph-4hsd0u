## 🎯 Personal Quests

> [!note] Bramble Paths
> The Paths of Elderglen Hollow run to many realities, and none leads to Amphoreus. On the way here Sir Caerwyn made it part of the trip to establish Path connections to this land; only crossing the land itself will show how thin the Bramble runs here. The trip is also Vireloth's: for once his companion calls the shots, which was the whole point of coming. Okhema's children, meanwhile, have decided Vireloth eats babies. A stall in the lower market sells dread-horse charms, and Sir Caerwyn's corrections are received politely and believed by no one.
>
> - [ ] Find where the Bramble runs thin in Amphoreus.
> - [ ] Establish a Path between Amphoreus and Elderglen Hollow.
> - [ ] Give Vireloth the adventure Vireloth wanted.
> - [ ] Clear Vireloth's good name.

> [!note] The End of the Road
> After the address, Castorice tried to sense Sir Caerwyn's death and couldn't, because he has already died once. Mayuri's sight showed him himself in blue flame and in white, and once as a giant moving half a step behind. He believes he is a fragment of Pharasma's will, and wonders what Thanatos has to do with it. Castorice told him that when he reaches the end of his road and doesn't know where else to go, he should come to her.
>
> - [ ] Learn what the blue flame and the white mean.
> - [ ] Learn what Thanatos is to Pharasma's will.
> - [ ] When the road runs out, go to Castorice.
