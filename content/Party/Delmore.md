## 🎯 Personal Quests

> [!note] A Name That Carries Weight
> Delmore grew up among wandering artisans who sold tools and trinkets from town to town and were looked down on for it. He wants more: a name known for the quality of what he makes, and a workshop of his own.
>
> - [ ] Make your name known for the quality of your work.
> - [ ] Open a workshop of your own.

> [!note] The Living Sample
> **Location:** The Philoxenion
>
> Delmore's tree sample won't die. Cut a section and it beads sap and reseals within the hour. Standard reagents come back contradictory, as if they can't decide what it is, and the sap binds like nothing in his formulary. Naming it will take a better library or a better expert than he has. His void shroom, meanwhile, eats light, slowly drains whoever handles it, and makes a remarkable base for darkness and slow toxins. He's keeping that research behind closed shutters.
>
> - [x] Study the sample.
> - [ ] Find a library or an expert who can identify it.
> - [ ] Turn the void shroom concept into a working formula.
