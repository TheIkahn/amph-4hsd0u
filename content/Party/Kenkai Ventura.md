## 🎯 Personal Quests

> [!note] On His Own Strength
> Kenkai set out to become strong enough to survive on his own, and to earn his place in the world through his own strength, whatever it takes. The Gloombeast nearly killed him, and he told Thessil Vane he means to get back the strength he had before that fight. In Okhema his name has started to carry: the charity run made it known in the neighbourhoods it served. At the Philoxenion he struck a bargain with an Ent: a boss's soul for a ring of speech.
>
> - [ ] Get back the strength you had before the Gloombeast.
> - [ ] Earn your place in the world through your own strength.
> - [ ] Bring the Ent a boss's soul for the ring of speech.

> [!note] The Prophet of Regret
> **Location:** On the road to Okhema
>
> In the Temple of the Three Fates, Metanoros, a Dawnspeaker who had kept the temple for four hundred years in Kephale's name, called the creatures of the night down on us. Afterwards Kenkai talked him down and told him to stop and go get help, and Metanoros started walking to Okhema. Back in the city, Kenkai searched the graveyard outskirts and found no sign of him, only a bear-sized, oozing sack of living creatures. Yuichi judged it the work of someone evil; Zuzros dispelled it from a distance and the animals scattered.
>
